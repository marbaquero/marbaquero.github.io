Questions

What families ask before starting.

Does my caregiver need to download an app?

No. She opens a link on her phone and fills a short form. If she can send a WhatsApp message, she can use Cuidar a Distancia.

Does it work if I live in another country?

Yes. It works anywhere there's a phone and internet. It was first used between Houston and Quito, and today there are families in the US, Spain and Latin America.

Can my siblings use it?

Yes. Invite every family member you want. Each person signs in with their own account and sees the same information, so there are no longer three versions of the same week.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The trial starts when your caregiver files the first real report, not when someone clicks a button. Those 14 days are for proving it works, not for watching a countdown.

What happens after 14 days?

You choose the Family plan at $49 a month. Cancel anytime, from the account or on WhatsApp. No contract, no penalty.

Does it replace the caregiver or the doctor?

No. It gives you the information to coordinate care and gives the caregiver clarity on what to do. Clinical decisions belong to your family member's health professionals.

Is my family's data safe?

Each family has its own space, and only the people you invite can see it. We don't sell data or show advertising.

What if my caregiver isn't comfortable with technology?

It's the most common objection. We train her by video call or WhatsApp, in her language, and the first report is filed during the setup call. If she can't, we find a simpler way together.

Questions

What families ask before starting.

Does my caregiver need to know about technology?

No. She opens a link on her phone and fills a short form. If she can send a WhatsApp message, she can use it.

Does it work if I live in another country?

Yes. It works anywhere there's a phone and internet. It was first used between Houston and Quito, and today there are families in the US, Spain and Latin America.

Can my siblings use it?

Yes. Invite every family member you want. Each person signs in with their own account and sees the same information.

What happens after 14 days?

You choose the Family plan at $49 a month. Cancel anytime, from the account or on WhatsApp.

Does it replace the caregiver or the doctor?

No. It gives you the information to coordinate care. Clinical decisions belong to your family member's health professionals.

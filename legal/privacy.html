This Policy explains how Cuidar a Distancia collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Service.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and household: name, email, password hash, household name, language, time zone, role, and subscription status.
  • Care recipient and care records: the loved one’s display name, daily reports, cognitive-session notes, medication and inventory records, and related notes you choose to enter.
  • Caregivers: name, optional phone number, token status, and the records they submit.
  • Expenses and payments: household expense entries, receipts you upload, caregiver payment records, and Stripe payment metadata. We do not store full card numbers; Stripe handles card data.
  • Technical: IP address, device/browser information, logs, and cookies needed for authentication and security.

Some care records may concern health or medication. We treat them as sensitive and limit access to the household and authorized caregivers.

2. How we use information

We use it to provide and secure the Service, authenticate users, resolve household data, process subscriptions, send transactional email, provide support, monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or use care records for advertising.

3. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on performance of a contract, legitimate interests (security, reliability, support), consent (where required), and legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis.

4. Sharing and processors

We share information with service providers that help us operate the Service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and edge functions.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and security for the portal and apps.
  • Stripe — payments, subscriptions, invoices, and the customer billing portal.
  • Email/SMS providers — transactional messages, if configured.
  • Monitoring/error tracking — reliability and security, if configured.

They process information under their terms and, where required, data-processing agreements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security. A business transfer may transfer information subject to this Policy.

5. International transfers

Our processors may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

6. Retention

We keep account and household data while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Care records are retained according to the household’s settings and our backup cycle. You may request deletion; some records may be retained where law requires.

7. Security

We use access controls, row-level security, encryption in transit, and limited administrative access. No system is perfectly secure; protect your password, enable MFA where available, and revoke caregiver links you no longer need.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection, and may withdraw consent. Contact hola@cuidaradistancia.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity and household authority before acting.

9. Children

The Service is intended for adults coordinating care. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect account data from children.

10. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies/local storage for authentication and language. If we add analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this Policy and, where required, ask for consent.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted with a new effective date and, where required, notified by email.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Company: Cuidar a Distancia
Contact: hola@cuidaradistancia.com

1. Monthly subscriptions

Family Plan and Full Setup monthly subscriptions renew automatically. You may cancel at any time from the family portal (“Manage billing”) or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future renewals. Access continues until the end of the current paid period, unless the account is terminated for breach.

2. Refunds — 30 days, no questions asked

If the Service is not useful to your family, write to hola@cuidaradistancia.com within 30 days of a charge and we will refund it in full. No reason is required and no questions are asked.

The refund covers every charge made in that period, including the one-time Full Setup fee. We process it within 10 business days, using the same payment method you paid with.

3. Full Setup fee

The one-time Full Setup fee covers data migration, configuration and 1:1 caregiver training. It is charged once, on the first invoice, and never renews.

It is covered by the same 30-day guarantee: if the setup does not work for your family, it is refunded with the first month.

4. Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before opening a chargeback so we can resolve the issue. Accounts with an open payment dispute or fraud review may have app access disabled while the matter is reviewed.

5. Cancellation and data

After cancellation, access ends at the end of the paid period. We retain data according to the Privacy Policy and backups. You may request an export before cancellation. We may delete data after the retention period.

6. Changes

We may update this Policy with a new effective date. Changes do not retroactively affect charges already made.
